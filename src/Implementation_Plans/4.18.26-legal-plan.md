# Implementation Plan: 4.18.26-legal-plan.md
## Technical & Legal Pivot: Carrillo Dynamics LLC

### 1. Objective
Perform a final technical and legal audit of the Carrillo Dynamics platform to mitigate legal exposure, centralize SEO for AI-Era Optimization (AEO/GEO), and finalize the transition to "Carrillo Dynamics LLC" with a new industrial grit branding.

### 2. Branding & Terminology Pivot
- **Company Name**: "Carrillo Dynamics LLC" (Updated in Footer, FAQ, and Legal/Privacy pages).
- **Core Slogan**:
  - English: "Engineered Precision. Industrial Grit."
  - Spanish: "Precisión de Ingeniería. Tesón Industrial."
- **Service Name**: Transition from "Forensic Audit" to **"Automation Blueprint"**.
- **Tone Softening**: Replace definitive guarantees ("Eliminate", "Ensure ROI") with operational descriptions ("Automate", "Minimize", "Drive measurable ROI").
- **Core Value (Spanish)**: "Tesón" (Industrial Grit/Persistence).

### 3. SEO & AEO/GEO Injection
- **Centralized Metadata**: `SEOManager.tsx` handles all page titles and canonicals.
- **AI/LLM Optimization**:
  - **LocalBusiness Schema**: Injected "Serving the Greater Chicago Area" and "Chicago-based Systems Engineering" into `StructuredData.tsx`.
  - **FAQPage Schema**: Semantic Q&A pairs added for LLM/GPT scrapers.
  - **Speakable Schema**: Accessibility markers for AI readers.
- **URL Sanitization**: Global removal of `/:lang` segments. Unified root domain indexing strategy.

### 4. Technical Architecture Changes
- **Language Management**: `LanguageProvider.tsx` managed via `localStorage` and internal React state. No page reloads or URL segments.
- **Routing**: Removal of all language-prefixed routes in `App.tsx` and all internal Link components.

### 5. Verification Checklist
- [ ] No `/:lang` segments in any internal `Link` or `window.location`.
- [ ] Page titles follow pattern: `Carrillo Dynamics | [Page Name] | Engineered Precision. Industrial Grit.`
- [ ] Footer displays "Carrillo Dynamics LLC".
- [ ] "Tesón" defined as a Core Value in Spanish Schema.
- [ ] robots.txt and sitemap.xml point to root URLs.

### 6. Legal Ledger
- **Status**: Transitioned to LLC.
- **Claim Strategy**: Operational velocity focus (Engineered approach) rather than deterministic guarantees.
- **IP Ownership**: Standardized at 100% Client Ownership.
